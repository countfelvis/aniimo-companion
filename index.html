Import & export

Build-tab screenshot scanning uses on-device OCR for landscape Capability Awakening images. Review all detected values before saving; images are not uploaded to our server.

Storage & privacy

Your edits auto-save in this browser using local storage. Clearing site data may erase them. Export a backup regularly. Accounts, cloud sync, and automated game-data updates are not available. OCR downloads its recognition engine on first use.

Artwork

Choose each Prismana’s Sparkling color in its Appearance tab. Paste a direct image URL or select a saved image from your device. The appearance gallery supports built-in images through the art manifest, plus personal overrides. Scorchhowl and Inferlupa each include 13 built-in appearances. Held-item artwork includes 27 screenshot-sourced icons; newly cataloged items start at zero owned until you update inventory. Other Prismana appearances are awaiting artwork.
